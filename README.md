# THE WORLD TODAY — Wednesday 25 November 2026

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261125)

One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn
entirely by `generate.py` (Python + Pillow, no network, fully deterministic).

**Lead story:** hackers breached an unclassified network the CIA and the
Director of National Intelligence use to analyse open-source material —
officials say no classified data was hit and the attacker is unknown.

**Also in the picture:** a Houthi drone strike on Riyadh's PIF Tower (11
wounded); Zelenskiy confirms Ukraine hit the Russian ship Sparta II in the
Gulf of Finland; Huawei found guilty in a US trade-secrets trial; the WHO
pulls its Ebola team out of Ituri, DR Congo; a Turkish army helicopter crash;
Sarkozy's corruption case goes to trial; Trump awards Giuliani the Medal of
Freedom; England level the T20 series; and a quasar from the early universe.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. Running `generate.py` twice produces byte-identical
images.
